# Bends

**Bends** lets you bridge virtual circuit traces with your fingertips to bleed, starve, and corrupt audio inside a live loop.

Digital imperfection is its core concept. Inspired by 90s circuit-bent rack units, Bends exposes the artifacts, memory corruption, and data bus shorting of early digital processors. It functions both as a stereo multi-effects processor and as a self-contained generative sound source.

## Origins & Residency

Bends was originally conceived as a card for the [Music Thing Modular Workshop Computer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-rACWw0J_zI&t=6s).

During the Synthux Academy Residency, I am expanding the concept into a standalone instrument and effects processor—exploring circuit bending without the risk of letting out the magic smoke.

Drawing on Reed Ghazala's concept of "body contacts", the hardware uses touch pads and conductive objects (fingers, coins, clips) to short virtual circuits, clock lines, and audio buffers.
